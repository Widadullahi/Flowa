# FLOWA: Conversation to Completion

Build a production-quality responsive landing page for an AI commerce operations platform called FLOWA.

BRAND:

Name: FLOWA

Meaning: From Conversation to Completion.

Primary tagline: "From Conversation to Completion."

Supporting headline: "Your customers chat. FLOWA handles the rest."

PRODUCT:

FLOWA is an AI-powered commerce operations assistant for businesses that sell through messaging, especially WhatsApp.

FLOWA turns natural customer conversations into structured orders and coordinates the operational journey from order capture to payment, fulfillment, delivery, tracking and completion.

Core positioning:

"FLOWA sits between customer conversations and business infrastructure."

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT position FLOWA as simply an AI chatbot.

Position it as an AI commerce operations/orchestration layer.

DESIGN:

Create a premium, minimal, modern SaaS aesthetic.

Use neutral colors.

Do NOT copy Xara's visual identity.

Do NOT use bright green as the dominant color.

Do NOT use generic AI purple.

Do NOT overuse gradients or glassmorphism.

Color palette:

Background: #F8F7F4

Primary text: #171717

Secondary text: #6B6B6B

Cards: #FFFFFF

Borders: #E8E6E1

Accent sage/forest: #6F8068

Dark section: #191A18

Typography:

Use a modern clean sans-serif such as Inter, Geist, or a similar premium sans-serif.

The page should feel trustworthy enough for business owners and polished enough for a startup/product competition.

FUNCTIONALITY:

- All CTA buttons labeled "Try FLOWA Now" should link to the FLOWA WhatsApp onboarding number/URL placeholder.

- Make the WhatsApp URL easy to replace through one configuration constant.

- Add smooth scrolling for internal sections.

- Add subtle animations on scroll, but keep them fast and professional.

- Make all cards responsive.

- Mobile-first design.

- The hero and product demo must look excellent on mobile.

- Use accessible semantic HTML.

- Buttons need clear hover/focus states.

- Do not use fake testimonials.

- Do not invent customer statistics.

- Do not claim FLOWA has certifications or partnerships that have not been confirmed.

- Use realistic demo data only where clearly presented as example/demo data.

The page should feel like a real startup product website, not a hackathon template.
